About

I am currently pursuing my master's degree in Nanoengineering and Nanoscience at National Taiwan University. My research focuses on advanced functional materials, electrocatalysis, anion exchange membrane water electrolysis, interfacial engineering, and plasma-assisted surface modification.

My current work aims to develop integrated catalyst–electrode architectures for efficient and durable green hydrogen production, with emphasis on catalyst–substrate interactions, porous transport layers, and process–performance correlations.

Research Interests

Anion Exchange Membrane Water Electrolysis

Development of high-performance and durable electrodes for AEM water electrolysis, focusing on device-level performance, interfacial resistance, mass transport, and operational stability.

Functional Nanomaterials and Interface Design

Design and synthesis of Ni-, Fe-, and Mo-based electrocatalysts with controlled morphology, surface chemistry, and catalyst–substrate interactions.

Plasma-Assisted Surface Modification

Surface engineering of electrodes using plasma-based treatment to tune wettability, surface defects, active-site reconstruction, and electrochemical reaction kinetics.

Selected Projects

NiMoO4 Nanowire Electrodes for AEMWE

Hydrothermal growth of NiMoO4 nanowires on stainless-steel fiber paper, carbon paper, and nickel fiber paper to investigate substrate-dependent nanostructure formation, OER activity, and AEMWE single-cell performance.

Self-Catalyzed NiFe Foam Anode

Development of a monolithic NiFe foam anode using a tape-casting-based process to eliminate the conventional catalyst-layer/porous-transport-layer interface and improve electrode durability.

Electrochemical Data Analysis

Python-based processing workflows for LSV, CV, EIS, Tafel analysis, iR correction, overpotential extraction, and batch comparison of electrocatalytic performance.
