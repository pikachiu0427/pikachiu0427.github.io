About

I am an R&D-oriented master's student in Nanoengineering and Nanoscience at National Taiwan University, with interdisciplinary training in materials science, nanotechnology, electrochemistry, and mechanical engineering.

My research focuses on advanced functional materials, electrocatalysis, anion exchange membrane water electrolysis, interfacial engineering, and catalyst–electrode integration. I am particularly interested in understanding how material design, substrate chemistry, and interfacial structure govern electrochemical performance and device-level durability.

I have published two first-author Q1 papers within 1.5 years, including one selected as a journal cover article. My long-term interests include advanced semiconductor processes, advanced packaging, metrology, energy materials, and R&D-driven process engineering.

Research Interests

Anion Exchange Membrane Water Electrolysis

Development of high-performance and durable electrodes for AEM water electrolysis, focusing on catalyst–substrate interactions, interfacial resistance, porous transport layers, mass transport, and device-level performance.

Functional Nanomaterials and Interface Design

Design and synthesis of Ni-, Fe-, and Mo-based nanostructured electrocatalysts with controlled morphology, surface chemistry, active-site reconstruction, and electrochemical activity.

Electrochemical Characterization and Mechanism Analysis

Investigation of reaction kinetics, charge-transfer behavior, electrochemical surface area, stability, and degradation mechanisms using LSV, CV, EIS, Tafel analysis, stability tests, and accelerated stress tests.

Advanced Manufacturing and Semiconductor-Oriented R&D

Application of materials analysis, mechanism-driven problem solving, process optimization, data analysis, and metrology concepts to advanced semiconductor process and packaging-related research.

Selected Projects

NiMoO4 Nanowire Electrodes for AEM Water Electrolysis

Hydrothermal growth of NiMoO4 nanowires on stainless-steel fiber paper, carbon paper, and nickel fiber paper to investigate substrate-dependent nanostructure formation, catalyst–substrate interactions, OER activity, and AEMWE single-cell performance.

Self-Catalyzed NiFe Foam Anode

Development of a monolithic NiFe foam anode using a tape-casting-based process. The integrated electrode architecture eliminates the conventional catalyst-layer/ porous-transport-layer interface and improves electrode functionality for oxygen evolution reaction and AEM water electrolysis.

Python-Based Electrochemical Data Analysis

Development of data-processing workflows for LSV, CV, EIS, Tafel analysis, iR correction, overpotential extraction, and batch comparison of electrocatalytic performance.

Plasma-Assisted Surface Modification

Exploration of plasma treatment as a surface engineering strategy to tune wettability, surface chemistry, defect structures, and electrochemical interfaces.
